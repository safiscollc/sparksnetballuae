About Sparks

A Club Built on Community, Not Just Competition

Sparks was never designed to be a coaching academy. From day one, the goal has been to create a club where people can play recreational netball, improve their skills, meet new people, stay active, and build real friendships.

We run regular sessions and community games, including at Al Salam Community School in Dubai, where players can join individually, get placed into a team, and play regardless of previous experience. It’s a space built on participation, confidence, and community first.

  • Play recreational netball
  • Improve your skills on court
  • Meet new people & build friendships
  • Join individually and get placed into a team
  • Play regardless of experience level

Our Mission

Growing Community Netball Across the UAE

Our mission is simple: give every player in the UAE a welcoming place to play, improve and belong, while building Sparks into a sustainable club for the long run. Here’s how we’re getting there.

  • Grow our membership across Dubai and the UAE
  • Run seasonal leagues and social competitions
  • Host community tournaments that bring players together
  • Partner with schools and corporate organisations
  • Launch official Sparks merchandise
  • Secure sponsorships that support our community
  • Become one of the leading community netball clubs in the UAE
